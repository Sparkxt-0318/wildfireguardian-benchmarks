## Open questions for the programme

1. **Which conventions are WildfireGuardian's?** This suite declares interval
   safety (WG-SEM-1) and opt-in waiting (WG-SEM-2). If a production component
   has chosen differently, the right response is a variant benchmark, not a
   weakened one — but somebody has to say which convention is the programme's.
2. **What is the acceptable-loss threshold?** The J family uses 10 in
   scenario-local units. A real threshold is a policy decision and would let the
   protectability benchmarks carry operational meaning.
3. **Is sup-regret used anywhere operationally?** WG-BM-036 shows it moving
   mechanically with ensemble size. If nothing consumes it, the benchmark is
   documentation; if something does, that consumer needs the ensemble provenance
   alongside the number.
